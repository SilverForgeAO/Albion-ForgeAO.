🎓 Crafting specializations & focus

Focus cost = base × 0.5^(efficiency ÷ 10 000). With a character selected above, efficiency comes from that character’s specializations (tab “Specializations”); the manual fields below are used only for “Manual values”. A filled “override” always wins. To get the exact value for an item, type the focus cost the game shows for it and press “Calibrate”.

Item enchanting – buy or craft, then enchant

Compares four routes: buy the base item and enchant, craft it and enchant, buy the enchanted item, or craft the enchanted item directly. Uses the buy/sell cities, RRR and fees from the settings bar. The material amount per item is an editable default by item slot – check it against the in-game enchanting window.

Specializations – crafting & refining

General (gold boxes) = mastery of a branch or sub-branch; blue-edged rows = specialization of one item (0–100). Everything is saved automatically in this browser, per character. Specializations only change focus efficiency – they do not change the return rate (RRR), which depends on the city and on using focus.
